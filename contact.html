Frequently Asked Questions
For Artists
Do I need blockchain experience to apply?
No. You do not need any prior experience with blockchain, Solana, or crypto to apply. Selected artists receive full support through the on-chain registration process as part of the exhibition programme.
What format should my work be submitted in?
Video works should be submitted as links (Vimeo preferred). Minimum 1080p, ideally 16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratio. Generative or interactive works should include a technical spec and demo link. Still image portfolios are accepted but video or generative formats are preferred for projection.

If the fee presents a genuine barrier, contact us at hello@meshahara.art before the deadline to discuss. We want the open call to be as accessible as possible.
Can collectives or groups apply?
Yes. Collective and collaborative submissions are welcome. Please designate one primary contact for correspondence and list all collaborators in the application form.
When will I hear back?
All applicants will be notified of the outcome by 10 September 2026. Selected artists will be contacted directly to discuss technical requirements and on-chain registration.
Do I need to be based in Malaysia or Southeast Asia?
No — the open call is international and open to artists from anywhere in the world. Southeast Asian artists are especially encouraged to apply, but there is no geographic restriction.
For Visitors
When and where is the exhibition?
Meshahara takes place at REX KL — Jalan Sultan, Chinatown, Kuala Lumpur. The event date is to be confirmed in 2026. Sign up to the newsletter for the announcement.
Do I need to know anything about crypto to attend?
No. The exhibition is designed to be fully accessible to non-crypto audiences. The wallet onboarding station gives visitors a hands-on introduction to Solana if they want it — but attending and experiencing the projection works requires no technical knowledge at all.
Is the event accessible?
REX KL is a ground-floor venue with step-free access from the street. For specific accessibility requirements, please contact hello@meshahara.art and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Can I take photos?
Photography and video for personal use is welcome throughout the evening. Commercial photography requires prior accreditation — contact hello@meshahara.art.
How do I get there?
REX KL is in Jalan Sultan, Chinatown. The nearest LRT station is Pasar Seni (5 minute walk). Grab works well for the area. Street parking is available in Chinatown.
