About the Curator

Independent practitioner.
Sole force behind
Meshahara 2026.

Nick Hersey is a British independent curator, producer, and digital artist based in Kuala Lumpur. Working under the moniker HERSOID, he operates at the intersection of digital art, cultural infrastructure, and community-building across Southeast Asia and beyond.

Meshahara: Many Roots, One Ledger was conceived, commissioned, and produced by Nick entirely independently — from initial concept through artist selection, venue negotiation, technical production, programme, publication, and opening night. Fifteen international artists. One night. One room. Delivered without institutional backing.

His curatorial practice is rooted in a career spanning favela youth arts in São Paulo, displaced Burmese migrant communities in Thailand, post-industrial cultural projects in the UK, digital and blockchain practice in Shanghai, and the emerging digital art ecosystem of Southeast Asia.

What Independent Production Means

Meshahara was not the output of a committee, a funded arts organisation, or an institutional brief. Every decision — curatorial, logistical, technical, financial — was made by one person, with full accountability and full creative authority.

That is a specific capability. It means a future collaborator gets a partner who knows how to do everything, who doesn't lose weeks to approval chains, and who treats every artist and every venue with direct, personal care. It also means a track record: not of managing upward, but of making things happen.

The result speaks for itself: fifteen international artists, architectural-scale projection at one of Southeast Asia's most distinctive venues, a coherent curatorial thesis, and a night that worked.
