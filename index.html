Curatorial Brief

Many Roots,
One Ledger

Meshahara takes its name from the Arabic root meaning to make public, to witness, to be seen. The exhibition asks what it means to share infrastructure without surrendering identity — how different cultural voices can occupy the same ledger without being erased by it.
