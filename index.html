About the Exhibition

Meshahara is a Solana-powered immersive exhibition framing blockchain not as financial speculation, but as shared cultural infrastructure — a decentralised commons where artists from Southeast Asia and the world circulate work, maintain authorship, and collaborate across borders without losing their identity.

At a time of rising nationalism and cultural isolation, the project positions art as a space for coexistence. Visitors don't scroll past the works — they inhabit them, entering a communal environment of large-scale projections where presence, not transaction, is the point.
